Staff and the admin console

Staff run the server from Admin in the account menu. It needs accounts (XMAGE_AUTH=true), and each staff member signs in with their own.

Roles

RoleCan
ModeratorSee who is online, mute and sign players out, watch any running game, work through reports and client errors, and read the audit log and server stats.
AdminAlso lock and deactivate accounts, remove tables, broadcast, decide who can register (Access), change Settings, and see Single player and Updates.
OwnerAlso give and take staff roles, plan maintenance, add and remove content packs, and update or roll back the server.

The account named in XMAGE_OWNER is always an owner. Give the other roles from the Staff tab. Roles are checked on every action, so taking one away works at once.

Console sign-in

When no owner can sign in, Console sign-in at the bottom of /admin opens the console as an owner with XMAGE_ADMIN_PASSWORD. It's a break-glass key: once staff have roles you can leave the password empty, which turns console sign-in off.

Maintenance

Owners announce maintenance from Settings, now or up to a day ahead, with a message. From then on no new tables start, and everyone online is warned again 30, 10, 5 and 1 minutes before. With restart checked, the server restarts once the time has come and no game is running. The Update button does this for you during an update.

Single player

The Single player tab shows the batch runner, which plays the AI-against-AI games players queue to test decks: what's playing, each player's daily quota and the queue, with a button to stop a batch. How many batch games run at once (default 1, and 0 stops the runner) and how many each player gets a day (default 200) are in Settings.

The same tab lists content packs: campaigns, puzzles, weekly challenges and opponents added without a new build. A pack is a zip with pack.json and its decks under decks/. Owners upload one there, or put the folder in db/content-packs/ and press Reload. A pack with problems stays out and says why. Packs are in the nightly backup.

The audit log

Every staff action that changes something goes in the Audit tab, with who did it and the reason they gave: mutes, locks, role changes, maintenance, updates and rollbacks.

Accounts, reports, feedback, client errors and the support ledger are described in the deployment guide.
