Career and single-player modes

Playmat's Career is a multi-track single-player hub spanning 60-card Constructed, 100-card Commander, Limited, and tabletop PvE variants — all free to play with zero real-money gating.

Multi-track onboarding and deck building

  • Starting tracks: New Career accounts pick either a 60-card Constructed starter or one of six 100-card Commander precons. Existing accounts receive a one-time free Commander precon claim, and additional precon bundles can be bought with Career coins in the Shop.
  • Format-aware Career decks: Decks saved in career_decks store their format (Constructed, Commander, Brawl). Editing a Commander deck enforces 100-card singleton rules and exposes color-identity filtering and a Pick Commander quick filter in the collection binder.
  • Quest focus: Players can set a quest track preference (any, constructed, or commander) so daily and weekly quests match how they play.

Commander Career core

  • Commander Precon Upgrade League: Start with 1 Commander precon and 6 packs in league.json, build a singleton deck from your league pool, and play either 1v1 duels or 4-player pods toward the same Commander League progression.
  • Commander Roster & Tables: Five tiers of 20 curated Commander encounters (commander-opponents.json), progressing from kitchen-table 1v1 duels into themed 4-player pods and asymmetric raid bosses.
  • Commander AI Ranked & Bracket Protection: Queue in 1v1 Commander Duel or 4-Player Commander Pod with placement movement (1st +2 pips, 2nd +1/safe, 3rd safe/-1, 4th -1). Bronze and Silver pods automatically restrict matchmaking to precon/upgraded brackets so new players are never blown out by high-power combo decks.

Roguelike Gauntlet and PvE variants

  • Commanders of the Spire (Commander Gauntlet): Draft 1 of 3 Legendary Commanders with a starting Command Relic and 2 of 4 color-matched singleton packs, fight 3-player Skirmish Elites, cut weak cards at Rest sites, and forge rare bombs at the Foil Forge across 27 relics and 20+ themed bosses.
  • Hordes 2.0: Ten themed Horde encounters across Normal, Heroic, and Mythic difficulties. Bring any personal, Career, Commander, or Meta deck; Commander decks automatically scale to 40 life and 12 waves, with an optional Endless Waves mode.
  • Archenemy: Twenty-four Schemes (schemes.json) powering both Hunt the Archenemy (60-life Scheme boss) and Become the Archenemy (1v3 with your own 20-card Scheme deck), complete with a battlefield Scheme reveal banner.
  • Planechase: Toggle XMage's built-in 21 Planes in Gauntlet, Weekly Challenges, Archenemy, and Two-Headed Giant. A collapsible battlefield Plane banner displays active static/chaos text and a sorcery-speed Roll Planar Die button.
  • Two-Headed Giant (Solo): Pair up with one of six selectable AI Partner archetypes (Constructed 30 life or Commander 40 life) against opposing 2-head pairs, with team-aware AI combat targeting and shared victory conditions.
  • Story Campaigns, Weekly Challenges & Puzzles: Narrative sagas including Five Paths, Guilds of the Crossroads (multicolor Constructed), and The Table of Four (Commander), alongside 24 rotating Weekly Challenges and 35 tactical/Commander puzzles.

Companion Bond evolution and cosmetics

  • Bond Tiers 1 to 5: Finishing counted games with a pet or companion equipped earns Bond XP in pet_progress (Migration 12). Tier 2 unlocks reactive table emotes on Commander casts, clutch low-life survival, and wins; Tiers 3 and 4 unlock 3D/companion accessories (Gold Crown, Cool Shades, Wizard Hat, Mana Collar); Tier 5 unlocks the Astral Aura and the exclusive Mythic Bond title.
  • Earnable 3D companions: Alongside the Party Corgi and Debug Duck perk pets, the Clockwork Owl, Ember Hatchling, and Rune Golem Pup render as articulated low-poly 3D companions in PetScene.tsx.
  • Command-Zone frames: Equip gilded Command-Zone borders (Gold Filigree, Phyrexian Gilded, Ravnica Guildcrest, Eldrazi Void, Theros Constellation, plus Atelier frames) synced via PlayerLook.commandFrame.
  • Coin Cosmetics Boutique & Gacha Waves 1 to 5: Buy playmats, sleeves, avatars, frames, companions, emotes, and titles directly with Career coins — with six deterministic Curator's Picks discounted 20% each UTC ISO week — or pull duplicate-free cosmetics from five Capsule machines (Full Art, Commander Vault, Planar Horizons, Tabletop Atelier, and Legends Showcase).
