Archbench & MTG AI Landscape

AI benchmarks & leaderboard.

Playmat measures every search change and self-play neural network on fixed, seeded Magic: The Gathering duels and tactical suites under Archbench. Below are our recorded model standings, our zero-shot results on unseen decks, and a research comparison with the strongest other Magic AIs out there: rules-engine bots, open-source RL projects, and frontier LLMs.

Different engines and benchmarks use different decks and rating pools. Where AIs have not played on the same board, we keep their rating pools separate and state the harness plainly instead of merging incompatible numbers.

    Speed, cost & Java XMage comparison

    Strength is only half the story for a playable Magic server. Dedicated Rust search and local neural models cost $0 per game and think in milliseconds to seconds; frontier LLMs can read any card text zero-shot, at 10 to 28 minutes of thinking time and $0.44 to $8.93 per game.

    Archbench suites & methodology

    Every suite in archbench/ is content-locked with deck fingerprints. Changing a deck, deal order, mulligan rule or turn cap creates a new version, never an edit, so older ratings stay comparable.

    How we measure fairly

    • Duplicate seat-swapped deals: each deal is played twice per pair of entrants with seats swapped so shuffle luck cancels out.
    • Strict imperfect information (hide=1): neural entrants never see the opponent's hand or library order. An automated leakage test resamples all hidden cards at hundreds of decisions and asserts the observation tensor is bit-identical.
    • Bradley-Terry MLE ratings: ratings on the Elo scale (400 / ln 10) with observed Fisher information 95% confidence intervals, plus trinomial SPRT gates for promotions.
    • Strictly unseen OOD decks: constructed-ood@1 (38 Modern, Limited and Brawl decks) was held 100% out of all 18 Phase 4 self-play rounds to measure genuine generalization.

    Adapters & cross-AI harness

    • Universal external protocol (playmat-ext-v1): PolicyKind::External streams zero-leakage board state and legal choices over NDJSON TCP or subprocess stdio (ext:tcp://..., ext:cmd:...), with optional ?obs=1 numeric tensors for neural models.
    • Jev & frontier/open-weight LLMs: Ready-to-run adapters in rust/rl/adapters/ cover TypeSafe AI's Jev (OpenRouter Decisions API), OpenRouter/OpenAI/Anthropic/Gemini models, and $0 local Ollama/vLLM models, tracking latency, tokens, USD cost, and fallback rate.
    • Open MTG AIs & harnesses: Includes ONNX Runtime (onnx:), Python plugins for MageZero / MTG-Causal-RL (python:), Forge-style heuristics (open:forge-heuristic), HTTP agents, mage-bench-style MCP bridges, and Forge/XMage CLI wrappers.
    • One-command benchmarking: rust/rl/benchmark_harness.py runs any mix of Playmat models and external adapters on constructed@2, constructed-ood@1, and pro-decisions@1, with --publish-to-site to update this page.
