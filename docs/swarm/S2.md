# Mana@Home S2: trust and credit

S2 of [the design](Design.md): autonomous replay and re-run verification as `verify` work units, trust tiers and
probation, per-worker statistical residual checks, automatic quarantine and credit freezing, Folding@home-style
credits, and the admin verification inbox. S1's record is in [S1.md](S1.md).

## What S2 built

- **Trust tiers and leasing rules** (`playmat-swarm/src/trust.rs`, `src/lib.rs`):
  - `probation` (default for new worker tokens, plus per-host probation for each new host on an account): 100% of
    the first 20 accepted units are audited by full re-run.
  - `normal` (automatic after 20 accepted units with 0 mismatches): ~3% random re-run audit (drawn from OS
    randomness so workers cannot predict audits), replay on the rest.
  - `reputable` (automatic after 30 days and 2,000 accepted units with 0 mismatches): ~3% on self-play, 10% on
    gate/rating (`match`, `bench`) units; eligible to lease `verify`, `match` and `bench` units.
  - `trusted` (admin-granted): 0% re-run audit; executes `verify`, `match` and `bench` units.
  - `banned`: refused at `/hello` and `/lease` (`403 Forbidden`).
  - A verifier can never lease a `verify` unit originating from its own account.
- **Autonomous `verify` units** (`playmat-worker/src/swarm.rs`, `src/main.rs`):
  - Completing a unit automatically queues a `UnitKind::Verify` unit (`VerifyMode::Replay` or `VerifyMode::Rerun`)
    with fresh presigned GET URLs generated at lease time.
  - `playmat-worker run` executes both modes and reports to `POST /api/v1/units/{id}/verify-complete`:
    - `replay`: downloads every uploaded file, checks every file's sha256 against the claimed manifest, checks
      `w.meta.i32` row count, and replays every game from `answers.jsonl` with no search.
    - `rerun`: downloads and sha256-verifies every uploaded file, re-plays the unit from scratch, and compares every
      game's `log_sha256`, `steps_sha256`, outcome, turns, and every uploaded shard file's sha256 bit-for-bit.
  - Workers now write one `w.*` shard in sorted deal order instead of concatenating per-thread shards, so every
    uploaded shard column is bit-identical across any `--jobs` thread count.
- **Per-worker statistics** (`playmat-swarm/src/trust.rs`):
  - Each `GameSummary` records `policy_entropy` (mean Shannon entropy of recorded policy targets).
  - Each completed unit is compared against peer workers on the same round/kind across four residuals: learner win
    rate, mean turns per game, mean policy entropy, and (one-sided) suspiciously fast time per game.
  - Outliers (`|z| >= 4.0` or combined chi-squared `p < 1e-4`) open a `stat-flag` inbox item and raise that
    account's re-run rate to 100% for 7 days rather than auto-banning, so fast honest hardware passes re-runs cleanly.
- **Quarantine, credit freeze and admin inbox** (`playmat-swarm/src/admin.rs`):
  - A failed replay or re-run mismatch rejects the unit (0 credit), demotes the account back to probation,
    quarantines all of that account's units in untrained rounds (`trained = 0`), freezes its last 30 days of credits,
    notes any already-trained rounds affected for rollback review, and opens an item in `GET /api/v1/admin/inbox`.
  - Admins can resolve inbox items (`confirm-cheat`, `false-positive`, `order-rerun`, `dismiss`), adjust trust or
    credits (with a written reason), quarantine/restore by account or round, order extra re-runs, reissue failed or
    quarantined units, mark rounds trained, and read the filtered audit log (`GET /api/v1/admin/audit`).
- **Credits and leaderboard** (`playmat-swarm/src/trust.rs`, `src/admin.rs`):
  - Accepted units (including `verify` units) earn `unit.cost * min(1.5, sqrt(window / elapsed))`, Folding@home style.
  - Credits start `pending` and mature 7 days after acceptance (`matured`), leaving time for audits to freeze or
    revoke credit before cosmetics payout.
  - Public `GET /api/v1/leaderboard` exposes credit, accepted units, games, and verified units per account.
- **Database migration**: existing S1 SQLite databases migrate in place via idempotent `ALTER TABLE` statements in
  `Swarm::open`.

## The S2 gate: a planted cheater caught within 20 units

Verified by automated end-to-end tests against a real coordinator and strict `playmat-worker` binaries:

| Scenario | Test | Detection path | Units to catch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forged winner in manifest | `e2e_test::s2_gate_planted_cheaters_caught_by_real_verifier` | Real `playmat-worker` re-run mismatch | 1 |
| Fake search (`iters=1` vs `iters=4`, 100% legal games passing replay) | `e2e_test::s2_gate_planted_cheaters_caught_by_real_verifier` | Real `playmat-worker` probation re-run (`log_sha256` + `steps_sha256` mismatch) | 1 |
| Uploaded shard byte tampering (`w.target.f16` 1-byte flip, honest manifest hashes, same file size) | `e2e_test::s2_gate_planted_cheaters_caught_by_real_verifier` | Real `playmat-worker` download hash check (`file_mismatches`) | 1 |
| Post-probation sleeper cheater (`Normal` account switching to fake low-entropy fast search) | `s2_trust::sleeper_cheater_caught_within_20_units_and_quarantined` | Per-worker statistical residual flag -> forced 100% re-run -> mismatch + full untrained round quarantine | 3 |
| Honest cross-thread verification (2-thread worker vs 1-thread trusted verifier) | `e2e_test::s2_autonomous_rerun_verification_accepts_and_credits` | Bit-exact match on games and all 21 deal-sorted shard/answer files; both credited | 0 false positives |
